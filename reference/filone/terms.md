# Filone Managed Storage v1 terms

This offer is a **2.49 USDFC per TiB per 30-day period Filecoin Boss add-on**. It is not an inclusive 4.99 USDFC storage product and does not change the price or recipient of the underlying FWSS storage service.

The add-on uses its own Filecoin Pay rail. The existing FWSS dataset and base-storage rail remain independently active and independently terminable.

Assurance is `CANCELLABLE_ONLY`: payment is bounded and the user can leave, but this offer does not claim an objectively verified SLA. Capacity-rate changes are prospective after synchronization; they are not retroactive historical measurements.

Payment authorization grants no ownership, custody, encryption key, or data-access authority. Any future data access requires a separate, explicit capability.

The payer's Filecoin Pay balance and aggregate rate determine account-wide runway. Tooling must disclose all recipients, caps, lockup, quote freshness, and the termination tail before consent.

A rendered offer or locally valid evidence document does not prove that any transaction occurred. Release evidence requires independent RPC, receipt, deployed-code, configuration, subgraph, SDK, CLI, and Explorer verification.
